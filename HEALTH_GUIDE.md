# Understanding Your Health Metrics in Ayumi 🍃

*A quick guide to making sense of the numbers on your screen, written just for you.*

If you've ever looked at a fitness tracker and thought, *"Wait, did I really burn 2,500 calories today just from walking?"* you're not alone. In Ayumi, we believe in giving you the full picture so you can make informed decisions about your health. 

Here is a breakdown of how we calculate the core metrics you see every day.

---

## 🔥 Active Calories (The "Extra" Burn)

**Active Calories** are the calories you burn *specifically* by moving your body above your normal resting state. 

Think of this as your "bonus" burn. When you go for a walk, take the stairs, or hit your daily step goal, you are generating Active Calories. 

In Ayumi, this number is calculated directly from your physical movement. If you spend the entire day lounging on the couch binge-watching your favorite show (we all need those days!), your Active Calories will stay very low.

**Why it matters:** Active Calories are the most direct reflection of your daily physical effort. If your goal is to be more active, this is the number you want to watch grow.

---

## ⚡ Total Calories (The Big Picture)

**Total Calories** represents *every single calorie* your body has burned today. This includes your Active Calories, but it also includes something very important: your **Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)**.

Your body is an incredible machine. Even when you are completely still—sleeping, breathing, or just keeping your organs functioning—it requires energy. Your BMR is the amount of energy your body needs just to stay alive. For most people, this accounts for the vast majority (about 60-75%) of the calories they burn every day!

**How Ayumi calculates this:**
When you enter your Biological Sex, Weight, Height, and Age in Settings, Ayumi uses a scientifically validated formula called the **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation** to determine your daily BMR. 

Instead of dumping all your maintenance calories onto your dashboard at once, Ayumi intelligently scales your BMR based on the time of day:
- At 12:00 PM (noon), Ayumi has added 50% of your daily maintenance calories to your total.
- By midnight, 100% of your daily maintenance calories have been factored in.

So:
**Total Calories = Active Calories + (Your BMR scaled to the current time of day)**

---

## 💧 Daily Hydration (Water Goal)

Staying hydrated is just as important as staying active. Water regulates your body temperature, lubricates your joints, and ensures your cells function properly.

**How Ayumi calculates this:**
While a generic "8 glasses a day" rule exists, hydration needs are highly personal. By default, Ayumi uses a standard clinical baseline to estimate your daily water needs:

**Weight (kg) × 35 ml**

For example, a person weighing 70kg would have an estimated goal of 2,450 ml (or roughly 2.5 Liters) per day. Ayumi also makes slight automated adjustments based on age and gender (for example, older adults generally require slightly less water relative to their body weight). 

If you haven't provided any physical stats, Ayumi will default to a sensible **3.0 Liters (3000 ml)** baseline goal. 

*Prefer to set your own goal?* You can always override this auto-calculated recommendation by setting a **Custom Water Goal** in the Settings screen!

---

### In Summary

*   **Look at Active Calories** when you want to measure your daily effort and movement.
*   **Look at Total Calories** when you are planning your meals or managing your weight.
*   **Watch your Water Goal** to ensure your body is properly fueled for both!

Keep stepping, keep moving, and most importantly, listen to your body! 🌿
